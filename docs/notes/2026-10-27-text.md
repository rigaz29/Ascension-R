# Roadmap step 5, first part: readable text, sRGB

The first-use screens now read as they should: gold text on black, the selected item in bright
yellow ("Adjust the slider so the left image is barely visible…", "Brightness", "Continue";
then "Screen Scale", "Screen Location X/Y", "Default").

## Which renderer

The port draws through the D3D12 vtable backend (`rsx_d3d12_backend.c`), the fork's default. The
live engine (`RSX_LIVE_DRAW=1`, the one ref B fixed for this title) registered its display
buffers but the presented image was the same to the pixel, so the work stays on the vtable path.
The shared pieces (`rsx_texture_layout.c`, `rsx_fp_decompiler.c`) already knew most of it: the X16
crossbar `0xAAEE` is even named in a comment there.

## Text as blocks

`TEXFMT_ALL=1` (new): every distinct texture a draw binds. The calibration screen binds `0xB4`
(X16, linear, 256x256, pitch 512), `0xBC` (X32_FLOAT 1280x720), `0xBF` (Y16_X16_FLOAT), besides
A8R8G8B8 and DXT. A dump of the X16 texture showed a clean distance-field font.

Three gaps, in order of discovery (fork `406202d7`):

1. `d3d12_bind_texture` recorded only B8, A8R8G8B8, G8B8, DXT and F16x4. The atlas was never
   bound and the glyph quads drew solid.
2. Bound, the glyphs vanished: the draw-time SRV picked its DXGI format from its own list and
   fell back to R8 over the R16 upload. Both now use `vp_tex_dxgi()`.
3. The glyph program computes its edge width as `max(|ddx|, |ddy|)` of the texture coordinate
   times 20 plus 0.02, then a smoothstep around 0.5. DDX/DDY were unhandled ops (destination 0).

## Dark brown instead of gold

The title sets `SET_SHADER_PACKER` (the RSX encodes integer colour outputs to sRGB) and puts the
gamma bits on its colour textures. Fork `a2b01654`: the packer is kept in `rsx_state`; a draw on an
integer target records `FP_CTRL_SRGB_OUT` in its control word (part of the pipeline key) and the
compile applies `rsx_fp_apply_srgb_output()`; the per-unit gamma masks go into the key and through
the new `rsx_fp_decompile_color_ex()`. `RSX_NO_SRGB=1` turns both off.

Checked: the calibration screen's "barely visible" checker peaks at (3,5,3) before and after; the
right half's lighter grey (16 to 18) is the screen's design, the "clearly visible" side. No
decompile or compile failure, the same 2.4 flips a second.
