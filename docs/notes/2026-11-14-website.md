# A website, and the repository's text for search (2026-11-14)

- **Why:** a search for "god of war ascension pc port" did not find the port. Google showed the repository as "GitHub -
  rigaz29/Ascension-R: A native Windows port of God of War: Ascension (PS3)...": the words "PC port" were in neither the
  title, the description nor the README's heading, and the results for those words are articles on the 2018 God of War's
  PC port. GitHub's traffic page had Google at 164 views in 14 days, for other words, and YouTube, X, pcmrace.com,
  thegamingemporium.com and Reddit above it.
- **The repository:** the description starts with "God of War: Ascension PC port"; four topics more (`unofficial-port`,
  `game-port`, `game-preservation`, `pc-gaming`, 14 in all); the website as its homepage; every release's title is
  "Ascension-R <tag>: God of War: Ascension PC port", the next ones too. The update notice reads the tags and the pages'
  addresses, which did not change.
- **The README:** its heading and first line say it is an unofficial PC port of God of War: Ascension, it links the
  website, and it has a FAQ: an official PC version, RPCS3, owning the game, which copies, Linux, safety.
- **The website,** `site/`, at https://rigaz29.github.io/Ascension-R/: one page with the title "God of War: Ascension PC
  Port – Ascension-R", a description, Open Graph and Twitter tags, schema.org data (`SoftwareApplication`, `FAQPage`),
  a canonical address and `sitemap.xml`. What it is, how it differs from an emulator, its features, status and needs,
  how to install it, and the same FAQ. No script, nothing that tracks, nothing of the game.
- **Published** by `.github/workflows/pages.yml` when `site/` or the two pictures change: it copies
  `docs/images/banner.png` and `icon.png` beside the page, as `check_repo.py` takes those two pictures in `docs/images`
  only. GitHub Pages is set to take its site from Actions. The actions are pinned to commits, as in `ci.yml`, at the
  majors of GitHub's own static-site workflow (configure-pages 5, upload-pages-artifact 3, deploy-pages 5).
- **Checked:** the page's JSON-LD parses, its questions are the page's, one `h1`, every tag closed; the live page is the
  file, byte for byte, and the pictures and the sitemap answer 200. Screenshots at 1280 and 500 px wide.
- **Left to the owner:** Google Search Console, a URL-prefix property for the website: its HTML tag goes in `site/index.html`'s
  head, then the sitemap is sent and the page's indexing asked for. Bing Webmaster Tools can take the property from Google.
  The social preview, `docs/images/banner.png` (1280x640, GitHub's size), is uploaded in the repository's settings: the
  API cannot.
