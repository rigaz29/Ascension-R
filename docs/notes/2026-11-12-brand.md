# The icon and the banner (2026-11-12)

- **What:** `docs/images/banner.png`, 1280x640, the README's banner and the repository's social preview;
  `docs/images/icon.png`, 512x512; `port/app.ico`, 16, 32, 48, 64 and 256, Ascension-R.exe's icon (`app.rc`).
- **Where they come from:** `scripts/brand/brand.py` builds `scripts/brand/brand.cpp`, which takes the precompile
  screen's scene and pixel font (`port/loading_screen.cpp`) as they are. The banner is the scene at 3 s with the
  lightning of 6.21 s moved 110 pixels left, behind the ruins and clear of the title; its top 12 rows are left off for
  2:1 and each pixel is 2x2. The title is the screen's font with a gold gradient, a dark outline and a shadow. The icon
  is a temple on a rock against the sun going down: 32, 48 and 64 from one layout, 16 drawn by hand pixel by pixel,
  where the lit edges would eat the temple; 256 and 512 are 64's pixels as squares. Nothing in them is the game's.
- **In git:** `check_repo.py` turns PNGs away and lets these two in by their path and SHA-256 (`OWN_PICTURES`), which
  `brand.py` prints when they change. An `.ico` is not a type it turns away.
- **The windows:** the launcher, the precompile screen and the game's window (the fork's `rsx_d3d12_backend.c`) take
  resource 1 as their icon and its 16-pixel image for the title bar; the game's window keeps Windows' own in a program
  that has none.
