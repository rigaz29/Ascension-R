# The launcher, rebuilt as a window of tabs

Written 2026-11-03. The user asked for a modern launcher with tabs, showing as an example a dark
launcher with a sidebar of tabs, a header picture, cards of settings and a big Play button. The
old one was Win32 group boxes, check boxes and combo boxes in one window. `launcher.cpp` now
draws its own, in this port's own design.

## What it shows

- **Sidebar:** the game's logo (`ICON0.PNG`), the build date, six tabs (Play, Graphics, Display,
  Controls, Game patches, System), and Open save folder at the bottom.
- **Play:**
  - a header with `PIC1.PNG` under a dark fade;
  - a ready check:
    - `GOWA.elf` at `[paths] elf`;
    - the profiles in the save folder (`*-PROFDATA-*`);
    - the GPU: the DXGI adapter with the most memory of its own, the one the backend took on
      the user's laptop (GTX 1650 Ti);
    - how many game patches are on;
    - a controller (XInput, checked every 2 s while the window is open), or keyboard and mouse;
    - the settings file;
  - the setup: three presets of the game patches, the window mode, the frame rate cap, VSync,
    and the frame rate and load on screen (`show_fps = overlay`; off is the title bar).
    - Smooth: Disable MLAA, Motion Blur and Shadows, the catalog's defaults.
    - Balanced: the same, the shadows kept.
    - Faithful: none.
    - "Custom patches" when the switches match none of them.
- **The other tabs:** the options as rows: a label, a line on what it does, and segmented
  buttons, a switch or a list. The patches' lines give the notes' measurements
  ([title patches](2026-11-01-title-patches.md), [shadows](2026-11-03-disable-shadows.md)).
- **The bar at the bottom:** the preset and a summary of the setup (or "Saved"), then Defaults,
  Save, Quit and PLAY. They do what they did.
- **Keys:**
  - Enter plays; Ctrl+S saves;
  - Tab, Shift+Tab, PgUp and PgDn change tabs;
  - Esc closes a list, the wheel scrolls it.
- **Keyboard keys** (Controls) opens the old keys window, now with a dark title bar.

## How it is built

- **Drawing:** Direct2D and DirectWrite on the window. WIC reads the PNGs, DXGI lists the
  adapters, DWM gives the dark title bar. All of it comes with Windows; the libraries are named by
  `#pragma comment`.
- **Input:** each paint draws the whole window and records what can be clicked. A click acts on
  what it was both pressed and released on.
- **Values:** each option keeps its value as text (`Opt::cur`), not in a control. A value the
  lists lack, written by hand, is shown as it is and saved back unchanged.
- **Game art:** nothing of the game is in the repository. The pictures are read when the window
  opens, from `<paths.vfs>/PS3_GAME`. Without them the header is plain and the logo is text.
- **Size:**
  - the exe is per-monitor DPI aware, so the window is 1120 x 720 at the monitor's scale;
  - it is drawn smaller when the work area is: 1120 x 720 at 150% does not fit a 1080p screen;
  - `WM_DPICHANGED` resizes it on another monitor.
- **COM:** initialized apartment-threaded for WIC, and released before the game starts. The
  game's audio initializes it multithreaded.
- **Shots:** `GOWA_LAUNCHER_SHOT=<folder>` draws every tab into PNG files at 125%, without a
  window, then quits. A seventh file is the Display tab with a list open.
- **Gone:**
  - the settings path under the groups: it is in the ready check now;
  - no link opens `gowa.ini`, because the launcher would write its own values over edits made
    while it is open.

## Verification

- Shots of every tab with `GOWA_LAUNCHER_SHOT`: nothing cut or overlapping, a long settings path
  cut in the middle.
- **The window, on the user's laptop screen (125%):**
  - It opened centred, at 1400 x 900 client pixels.
  - Clicks were posted to it, so the real mouse did not move. It ran on a copy of `gowa.ini`.
  - Graphics, Play, Balanced, Save: the copy changed in one line (`disable_shadows` on -> off).
    The bar said Saved, and the ready check counted 2 of 5 patches.
- **PLAY,** on a copy of `gowa.ini` whose `hdd0` was a copy of the saves:
  - The launcher closed and the game started: D3D12 on the GTX 1650 Ti, its system data and
    both profiles loaded, the title bar at 60 fps on the intro.
  - Stopped after 25 s.
- Not tested:
  - a second monitor at another scale;
  - the hover highlights with a real mouse (a posted move is undone by `WM_MOUSELEAVE`);
  - a controller plugged in while the window is open.
