# The evade on the right stick

Written 2026-11-07. The report: running, a flick of the right stick does nothing. It takes two, with a pause.

**In short**
- The pad path was right. The title reads the pad once a frame, 33 reads a second in play, and every read had a fresh packet.
  A scripted flick of 255 for 100 ms got through as three reads, and Kratos rolled.
- The title's evade is demanding: it smooths the right stick over its reads, wants ~0.87, and takes the pad's range for a square.
  A thumb's flick at 30 frames a second is two reads near the top, and a stick's circle does not reach the square's corners.
- Three changes, set by the port in `main.cpp` with `ps3_pad_tune(0.85, 4)`. Simulated thumbs went from almost never rolling to
  always. **Not tried on a real pad here.** That is the user's to confirm.

## What the evade wants

No need to find the code: the right stick was driven read by read at the gate, Kratos standing, with shots before and after each
flick (probes `r5_seq.py`, `r7_seq3.py`, `roll_table.py` in `logs/edge-ana/tools`, runs `logs/edge-ana/pad`).

| right stick X, read by read | rolled |
|---|---|
| 255 once or twice | no |
| 255 three or four times | yes, 4 of 4 |
| 250 or 252, four times | yes, 4 of 4 |
| 245 or 240, four times | no, 0 of 3 |
| 240 eight times | yes, 1 of 1 |
| 230 six times, 200 eight times | no |
| 170, 255, 255, 170 | no, 0 of 2 |
| 170, 255, 255, 255, 170 | yes, 2 of 2 |
| 170, 230, 255, 255, 200, 150 | yes, 2 of 2 |
| 0, 1 or 8 four times (left) | yes, 6 of 6 |
| 16 four times | 1 of 2 |

Halving the distance to the stick each read, with a threshold of ~0.87, gives all of these. How far it goes by frames and how
far by time was not measured.

Diagonals do not go by length. (217, 39) and its mirrors are 0.98 long and did not roll in three rounds of four directions, though
one (216, 40) may have. (235, 20) is 1.19 and did, and (247, 85) and (171, 9) at 20 and 70 degrees did. That fits the title
spreading the pad's square over a disc: the corners count for less.

The title's code: `func_00418340` calls `cellPadGetData` and returns 2 on `len` 0, `func_002CF2F0` keeps the last state on that,
and stores the sticks as 0-255 at +0x84 (left X) to +0x90 (right Y) of the pad object. What reads them was not looked for.

## Why it failed

- **The time.** A thumb is at the end of its travel for well under 100 ms. At 33 ms a read that is two reads, and two do nothing.
  What is left of the first flick in the smoothing is what the second one built on, hence "two, with a pause".
- **The range.** The conversion reached 255 only at the stick's mechanical end.
- **The shape.** A dead zone per axis and a circular gate left a diagonal at 0.61, 0.61.

## What changed

`libs/input/cellPad.c`, all three behind `ps3_pad_tune`, so other titles keep what they had.
- **`input.stick_full`, 0.85 here.** Full deflection at 85% of the travel, with a round dead zone, the circle spread over the square.
- **`input.flick_hold`, 4 reads here.** A right stick that reached 0.9 is held at its furthest for that many reads. The title
  uses the right stick for nothing else in play.
- **A thread polls the pad every 4 ms** between the title's reads. A read then gets the furthest the right stick went, and any
  button pressed since the last one, so a tap shorter than a frame counts too.
- `pad_now_ms` moved off `GetTickCount64`, which steps every 15.6 ms: the 4 ms interval was really 15.6.

## Results

A thumb simulated at the XInput level, in real time, so the sampling is the real one: rise, hold and fall of 40, 30 and 40 ms for
"110 ms". Gate, about 37 reads a second.

| flick | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| full, 110 ms | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| full, 70 ms | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| 90% of the travel, 110 ms | 0 of 5 | 5 of 5 |
| 80% of the travel, 110 ms | 0 of 4 | 4 of 4 |
| full, 180 ms | 3 of 4 | 5 of 5 |
| four diagonals, 110 ms | 0 of 5, one of them | 20 of 20 |
| 20 and 70 degrees, 110 ms | | 8 of 8 |

The first round of a run moved the picture by itself (~10 on the measure, a real roll 20-70), and was not counted as a roll.

**Not checked:** the left stick's feel with full deflection at 85% and the round dead zone; a real pad; the same at the cap of 30
reads a second, where a read is 33 ms and the hold 133 ms.
