# Cheats: infinite health and one hit kill

Written 2026-11-07. The launcher's Cheats tab, `[cheats] infinite_health` and `one_hit_kill`, both off by default. `cheats.cpp`,
called through `scripts/ppu_hooks.json`, so no instruction of the title changes and a game with both off runs the same code.

**In short**
- Health is a float at `creature + 0x214`, its maximum at `+ 0x218`. All damage goes through one function.
- Infinite health sets that damage to 0 for Kratos. One hit kill raises it above the victim's health for what Kratos hits.
- Tried in one scripted fight of the first level, on three parasites. Bosses, falls and scripted deaths have not been tried.

## How it was found

`tHealthMeter`'s RTTI led to its methods, and they to the object they feed: `func_00084598` clamps a float to 0 and `+0x218` and
stores it at `+0x214`, the setter. Only four functions store to `+0x214` of a creature:

| function | what it does |
|---|---|
| `func_00084598` | set the health (r3 the creature, f1 the value) |
| `func_00084EF8` | `goCreature`'s vtable entry 10: stores a value clamped to the maximum (not read closely) |
| `func_00087440` | **the damage** (r3 the creature, f1 the damage, r5 1 for a first pass on `+0x224` only) |
| `func_00087678` | a heal |

`func_00087440` has four callers:

| call | what it is |
|---|---|
| `0xC05CC` in `func_000BFC60` | a tick, with a negative amount: regeneration |
| `0x154558` in `func_00154460` | a hit from a message, the attacker in r31 |
| `0x156950` in `func_00156680` | a hit, the attacker in r31, the victim in r30 |
| `0x157C28` in `func_001573B8` | the same hit's first pass, r5 = 1 |

A creature's controller, at `+ 0x1304`, says what it is, by its vtable. In the fight:

| controller | vtable | who | health |
|---|---|---|---|
| `goPlayer` | `0x6925D0` | Kratos | 100 |
| `goAI` | `0x691930` | enemies | 10, 11 |
| `goPassiveAI` | `0x69EA78` | the level's props | 700, 10000 |
| `goNeoIO` | `0x69E920` | the level's props | 1, 10000 |

## What the hooks do

- `gowa_cheat_damage`, at the head of `func_00087440`: positive damage to Kratos becomes 0. Heals and regeneration pass.
- `gowa_cheat_hit`, at both calls with an attacker: when the attacker is Kratos and the victim is neither a player nor a prop, the
  damage becomes the victim's health plus its maximum plus 1. The props stay out because a script may need them alive: one of the
  10000 ones was set to 9960 in the fight, so something counts them down.

## Results

One run of the scripted parasite fight each, the damage logged by a probe that is not committed.

| | both off | both on |
|---|---|---|
| a parasite's hit on Kratos | 1.0 to 1.5 of his 100 | 1.0, health stays 100 |
| Kratos's hit on a parasite | 4 of its 10 | 21 of its 10 |
| hits on each of the three parasites | 3 | 1 |

In the run with both off, every drop in Kratos's health came through `func_00087440`: the six sets were all to 100.

## Not tried

- Bosses. Their damage can go to the threshold object at `+ 0x230` (`func_00223A50`, `func_00223A20`), and whether a script's
  phase survives a hit that large is not known. Turn One hit kill off if a boss stops responding.
- Falls, traps and scripted deaths, which may not go through the damage. Infinite health says hits for that reason.
- Weapons and magic other than the blades' combo: their hits may name something else than Kratos as the attacker, and then count
  as usual.
- Allies. A hit from an enemy on an ally is left alone.
